Institutional Trading Journal Features & Capabilities

Liber Journal is an open-source, privacy-first web terminal designed for professional traders, proprietary funds, and retail investors. Built with client-side cryptography and local IndexedDB database persistence, no trade information or financial records ever leave your computer.

  • Continuous Ledger: Comprehensive trade journal supporting multiple executions, commissions, swap, and automated R:R calculation.
  • Quantitative Risk Metrics: Automated computation of Expectancy, Sharpe Ratio, Profit Factor, Win/Loss Streaks, and Max Drawdown.
  • Interactive Visualizations: Recharts equity curves, monthly PnL heatmaps, and weekday distribution analysis.
  • Multi-Broker CSV Import: Direct parsing for MetaTrader 4, MetaTrader 5, Interactive Brokers, Binance, and Bybit.
  • Dynamic ExcelJS Export: Multi-tab Excel workbook with live dynamic formulas for institutional audit trails.
